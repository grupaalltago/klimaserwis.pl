# Domain: CRM

## Purpose

Manage relationships with customers, providers, and opportunities across the platform.

## Responsibilities (logical)

- Accounts, contacts, companies
- Pipelines and activities
- Interaction history linked to leads and jobs

## Non-responsibilities

- Public directory listings
- Inventory / invoicing depth → ERP
- Raw lead intake rules → Lead Generation (CRM consumes accepted leads)

## Dependencies

- Identity & tenancy (OD-005 — critical)
- Lead Generation events/ports
- Notifications

## Implementation status

Documentation only. No business logic in repository yet.
